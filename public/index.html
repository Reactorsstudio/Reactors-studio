Questions

Answers before you ask

Do I need any experience?

No. Classes are tailored to your experience and goals, from complete beginners to experienced performers.

When and where are classes?

Wednesday evenings, 7:00 to 9:30pm, at York Street Methodist Church, 25 York Street, Broadstairs CT10 1PS. The first term starts on 6 January 2027.

How big are the groups?

Groups are small, with a maximum of 20 people, so every voice is heard.

How long is a term?

Ten weeks, with sessions of two and a half hours.

How much does it cost?

You can pay £95 a month by subscription, or £295 for the full ten week term in one payment. Payment is taken securely through Stripe.

Can I cancel my monthly subscription?

Yes, at any time, with no cancellation fee. Your subscription stays active until the end of the billing period you have paid for. Read the full monthly subscription policy further down this page.

What if I can't make a class?

We cannot offer refunds or reductions for individual classes missed. If you can no longer attend, contact us as soon as possible and we may be able to transfer your booking to a future course.

What will we do in class?

You will explore a range of acting methods, from Stanislavski to improvisation, in classes that are energetic, playful and purposeful.

Is there a performance at the end?

Each term concludes with an end-of-term showcase or informal sharing, so you can celebrate your progress.

Policy

Booking, cancellation and refund policies

We want you to enjoy a welcoming, creative and supportive place to develop your acting. Please read the policy for the way you are paying before you enrol. Full term payment comes first, then the monthly subscription.

Full term payment (£295)

Places are limited to 20 participants per term.

1. Course fees and booking

Our 10-week acting course costs £295 per person, payable in full when you book. Your place is confirmed once we have received your payment.

2. Your right to cancel

If you book online, you will normally have a legal right to cancel within 14 days of making your booking and receive a full refund.

If you ask to begin attending classes during this 14-day period, you may be charged proportionately for any classes already provided if you then cancel. Your statutory consumer rights are unaffected by this policy.

3. Cancellations after 14 days

  • More than 28 days before the course begins: we refund any payments made.
  • 14 to 28 days before the course begins: we offer a 50% refund of the total course fee paid, up to a maximum refund of £147.50.
  • Less than 14 days before the course begins: we cannot normally offer a refund.

Unexpected circumstances arise, and we will always consider exceptional situations individually.

4. Missed classes

We cannot offer refunds or reductions for individual classes missed due to holidays, illness or other personal commitments.

5. If we cancel a course

  • If we cancel a course before it begins, you receive a full refund of all course fees paid.
  • If we cancel an individual session, we will try to arrange a replacement class. If that is not possible, we refund the appropriate proportion of your course fee.
  • If a course is discontinued after it has begun, we refund the proportion relating to classes not delivered.

6. Transferring your place

If you can no longer attend, please contact us as soon as possible. Where practical, we may be able to transfer your booking to a future course, subject to availability and agreement.

7. Contact us

Questions about bookings, cancellations or refunds? Call 07939 208234 or email reactorshq@gmail.com.

Monthly subscription (£95 a month)

Our monthly subscription lets you spread the cost of classes through convenient recurring payments. Please read these terms before subscribing.

1. Monthly subscription payments

  • Subscription fees are collected automatically each month through our secure payment provider, Stripe.
  • Your first payment is taken when you subscribe, unless otherwise stated at checkout.
  • Later payments are collected on your monthly renewal date.
  • Your subscription continues until you cancel it or it is otherwise ended under these terms.
  • The monthly price and payment schedule are shown clearly before you subscribe.

2. Cancelling your subscription

You may cancel your monthly subscription at any time.

  • There is no cancellation fee.
  • Cancellation prevents further monthly payments.
  • Your subscription stays active until the end of your current paid billing period.
  • To avoid your next payment, cancel before your next scheduled renewal date.

You can ask us to cancel, or use the subscription management link provided with your booking where available. We will confirm your cancellation in writing.

3. Refunds

Once your statutory cancellation period has expired, monthly subscription payments are generally non-refundable where the relevant classes or services have been made available as agreed.

If you cancel partway through a billing period, you may keep attending classes until the end of that period. We do not normally offer a partial refund for unused days or missed classes. We will consider exceptional circumstances individually and fairly.

4. Your statutory right to cancel

If you subscribe online, you will normally have a legal right to cancel within 14 days of entering into your subscription agreement, and you will generally be entitled to a refund.

If you have expressly asked for classes to begin during the 14-day period, we may deduct a proportionate amount for services already provided, where the law allows. Nothing in this policy affects your statutory consumer rights.

5. Missed classes

We cannot normally offer refunds or subscription reductions for classes missed because of holidays, illness, work commitments or other personal circumstances. Where possible, we may offer alternative arrangements at our discretion.

6. Classes cancelled by Re:Actors Studio

If we need to cancel a scheduled class, we will try to arrange a suitable replacement. If we cannot, we will offer an appropriate refund or credit for the class not delivered. If we permanently discontinue the subscription service, any fees paid in advance for services not provided will be refunded.

7. Changes to subscription fees

We may occasionally need to adjust our subscription fees. We will tell you in advance when a new price would take effect, and you can cancel your subscription before any increase applies.

8. Contact us

For cancellations, refund requests or payment queries, call 07939 208234 or email reactorshq@gmail.com. We aim to resolve all enquiries promptly, fairly and with understanding.
